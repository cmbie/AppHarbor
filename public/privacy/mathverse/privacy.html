1. Introduction

Welcome to MathVerse. MathVerse is an epic educational learning adventure designed to mae mathematics engaging, interactive, and fun for students.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable privacy frameworks, including consideration of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) .

2. Information We Collect

MathVerse is designed with student privacy as a core priority. Depending on how you interact with our platform, we may collect or store.

  • Profile Information: Usernames or nicknames chosen by the player and avatar preferences.
  • Device & Technical Data: Standard browser type, operating system, and basic non-identifiable usage diagnostics.

3. How We Use Information

Any information gathered within MathVerse is used strictly to enhance your gameplay and educational experience:

  • Save and load game progress, quests, and unlocked math arenas.
  • Display player stats, leaderboards, and achievements on your profile.
  • Power our virtual companion (Lex) to provide contextual learning assistance.
  • Maintain website performance, fix bugs, and optimize loading times.

4. Cookies & Technologies

We use essential cookies or local storage keys strictly necessary for core platform functionality (such as remembering your sound preferences or active game session). We do not use intrusive tracking cookies for behavioral advertising.

5.Third-Party Services

Standard server access logs may collect anonymized IP Addresses and request timestamps for security and uptime monitoring. We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third-party advertisers.

6. Children's Privacy

MathVerse is built for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as real full names, home addresses, phone numbers, or private emails) from children without parental or educator supervision. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided unnecessary personal data, please contact us for immediate review and deletion.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard the integrity of our platform. However, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
